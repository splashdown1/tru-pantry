TRU PANTRY

Offline canning card. USDA times only. No invented recipes.

Low-acid food (vegetables, meat, fish, dried beans, soup) is pressure-canner only. A boiling-water bath does not reach 240°F and does not control botulism. If a lid bulges, liquid spurts, or it smells wrong: do not taste. Discard.
Packed minutes are the USDA 0–1,000 ft column, reprinted from extension tables that cite the Complete Guide. Elevation box is feet above sea level. Mobile, Alabama is about 10 ft. Not a substitute for the full Guide.
