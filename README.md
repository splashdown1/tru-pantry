# TRU PANTRY

Offline, non-LLM **canning card**. One HTML file. No accounts, no telemetry, no generated process times.

**Live app (after Pages is on, branch main / root):** https://splashdown1.github.io/tru-pantry/

Lifeline keeps a body through the first days. Pantry is how food lasts after that, using only published USDA figures.

## Law

- Low-acid foods are pressure-canner only. A boiling-water bath is not a substitute.
- Packed minutes are the USDA 0–1,000 ft column.
- Boiling-water altitude step: +5 / +10 / +15 / +20 minutes across the Guide's elevation bands. Pressure canning raises pounds, not minutes.
- A food with no packed entry is a gap. The page will not invent a salsa or a family recipe.
- Bulge, spurt, mold, off smell: do not taste.

## Sources

USDA Complete Guide to Home Canning, as reprinted by the National Center for Home Food Preservation and University of Minnesota Extension canning tables. Tomato acidification is the Guide's bottled-lemon-juice / citric-acid rule.

Open `index.html` in any browser. Altitude is stored on the device.
